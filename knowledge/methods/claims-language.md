# Claims language

What the reviewer flags and what evidence clears it. This is a checking aid for a CSRD climate disclosure. It is not legal advice.

Two sources set the bar:
- **ESRS E1** for what a climate claim in the sustainability statement must rest on (gross targets, separate credits, 1.5°C basis).
- **Directive (EU) 2024/825** (empowering consumers for the green transition), which bans generic environmental claims without recognised excellent environmental performance, and claims that a product has a neutral, reduced, or positive impact based on offsetting. It applies to consumer-facing claims from 27 September 2026. The same wording in a sustainability statement is a signal the client's marketing may carry it too.

| Term or pattern | Evidence that clears it | If not met |
|---|---|---|
| carbon neutral, climate neutral, CO2 neutral, net zero (as achieved) | Not cleared by credits. Only by gross reductions plus permanent removals, stated in E1-9, and the term is about the company, not a product. | blocking |
| net zero target, net zero by YEAR | A gross reduction target in E1-6 plus an explanation of how residual emissions are permanently neutralised (E1 AR 11) | blocking |
| offset, compensated, balanced out | Credits reported only in E1-9, never netted from E1-8 or counted toward an E1-6 target (AR 12, AR 20(d)) | blocking |
| science-based, aligned with 1.5°C, Paris-aligned | E1-6 para 24(c) statement with comparison to a 1.5°C reference value (AR 17). Validation body named if claimed. | blocking |
| green, eco-friendly, sustainable, environmentally friendly, climate-friendly (generic) | A specific, quantified fact next to it, sourced to inputs | major |
| low-carbon, clean energy, renewable (as a share) | A figure from E1-7 with source | major |
| reduced emissions by X% | Base year, base value, current value, scope, and Scope 2 method, matching E1-6 and E1-8 | blocking |
| leading, best-in-class, industry-leading | A named benchmark and source | major |
| will, aims to, commits to, plans to | An approved plan, target, or body named in inputs | major |
| compliant, assured, audited, verified, certified | Only a named third party and the scope of its work, from inputs | blocking |

When a term is used in a quotation from a policy name or a third-party label, check the label exists in inputs before flagging.
